About

Engineering software with a taste for what happens beneath the abstraction layer.

I am a Software Engineering student at Politeknik Negeri Banyuwangi, focused on backend systems, database architecture, developer tools, and modern programming languages.

I like turning low-level curiosity into practical software: small databases, language experiments, reliable APIs, and tools that make development feel cleaner.

My interests move through compiler theory, system programming, distributed systems, database internals, concurrency models, and the craft of making software understandable.
